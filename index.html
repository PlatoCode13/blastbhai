ICB 2026 rules

Type both scores and the winner picks itself, or just tap the winner. Overs like 7.4 = 7 overs 4 balls; tap All out if a team lost 8 wickets (9 players a side). Then add the Man of the Match and share the poster.

Each match gets 3 officials from one Hamburg team that isn't playing, and a scorer from an outside team. Duties right before a team's own match are avoided first; officiating right after playing is allowed but kept even. Nobody ever officiates while playing.

Names per team

First names, separated by commas. Hamburg teams: at least 3 people. Outside teams: at least 1 scorer.
